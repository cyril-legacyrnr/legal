Privacy Policy – LegacyRnR QuickBooks Connector

Effective October 15, 2026

The LegacyRnR QuickBooks Connector (the "App") is a private, internal tool owned and operated by Legacy Group Global ("LegacyRnR", "we"). It is used only by our own staff to read and update our own company's QuickBooks Online books. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

What data the App accesses

With the authorization of our QuickBooks administrator, the App reads accounting data from our own QuickBooks Online company: the chart of accounts, transactions, vendors, customers and financial reports. It may post journal entries that our staff review and approve first.

How the data is used

  • Month-end reconciliation, financial reporting and bookkeeping for Legacy Group Global.
  • Nothing else. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any third-party purpose.

Storage and security

  • Access tokens and data stay on company-controlled computers and accounts.
  • Connections to Intuit use encrypted HTTPS.
  • Only authorized LegacyRnR staff can run the App.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share QuickBooks data with anyone, except our own accountants and tax advisers acting for Legacy Group Global, or when the law requires it.

Retention and revoking access

We keep data only as long as we need it for our books and records. The QuickBooks administrator can disconnect the App at any time in QuickBooks Online (Settings → Apps); disconnecting stops all further access immediately.
